# Wardrobe: cinematic launch film

`wardrobe-film.mp4`: 1920x1080, 30 fps, 16.8 s, H.264 BT.709, AAC, -14 LUFS. `poster.jpg` is also frame 0.

Built with cinetic 1.5.1 (Remotion, launch-simple template, "collage" direction) from the /brag work.
The film is one sentence carried by one element, the white tee:
a real person photographs her clothes -> the tee photo is cut out in steps by Wardrobe's real Smart AI -> it joins
every other piece (Tops / Bottoms) -> the ring of clothes dives onto "Style me" -> the week is planned on the phone
("Today: Weekend easy", "Mark as worn") -> the end card: the hanger mark, "Wardrobe", "One HTML file, no account."

Source project: `../film/wardrobe` (kept out of git; node_modules, renders and licensed media are local).
