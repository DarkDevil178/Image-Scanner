## Transitions
- 6.0 s, blur-through: the reveal blurs up and the cut happens at the blur's peak, onto cinetic's promise line clearing.
- 12.9 s, whip: the closet's own smear is carried out to the left and Try on slides in from the right, motion-blurred.
- 17.1 s, zoom-into: the camera pushes into the Try on phone and cuts inside the blur onto cinetic's phone macro pulling back.

## Vertical
Built natively, not cropped from the horizontal: the /brag scenes in a vertical layout, cinetic's promise, snap and
closet rendered at 1080x1920, its phone section cropped from a 4K render, and a vertical end card in the same design.
