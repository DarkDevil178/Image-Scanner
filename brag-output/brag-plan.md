# /brag plan: Wardrobe

**What it is:** A single-file mobile web app that turns photos of your clothes into a digital closet. It cuts out backgrounds, lets you swipe outfits together, styles you when you're stuck, and plans the week.
**Who it's for:** Anyone who stands in front of a full cupboard with "nothing to wear".
**What sets it apart:** It's one HTML file. No app store, no account. The cutout AI runs on the phone, and clothes are kept to real-world scale in Try on.
**Hook:** "Nothing to wear?" while clothes rain down on the beat.
**Tone:** default / app-store hybrid: punchy, clean, playful.
**Visual identity:** The app's own palette (#eceef2 background, #161922 ink, #3d4fe0 indigo accent) and its fonts (Bricolage Grotesque + DM Sans). Every phone screen is the real app, captured headless in Chromium.

## Notes
- The demo wardrobe (18 items, 6 outfits, a week of plans) is fictional: flat-lay garment illustrations loaded through the app's own backup restore. No real user data appears.
- The cutout scene is illustrative. The real Smart AI cutout downloads its model on first use, so it isn't run offline here; the editor around it is the real app.
